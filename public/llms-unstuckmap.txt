## What it is

Not advice, and it says so on the page: a map of options, not financial or legal
advice. The plan commits to an ORDER, because somebody stuck is usually not
short of information — they are short of knowing which thing is first.

Three moves. Each has a gate: the one fact that must become true before the next
move starts. "The sale has closed and you know what it cleared." "Three people
have paid you."

## Who it is for

Anyone who feels stuck and can describe their own situation. It was built for
people with ordinary money problems and real constraints — a mortgage, shift
work, kids at home, an aging parent, an inherited house, debt at a rate nobody
has explained to them. It assumes nothing about whether you run a business.

## What it costs

The map is free. Always.

The walkthrough for each move — the step-by-step, your questions answered, and
it keeping up as things change — is $29 a month, monthly only, cancel any time.
There is no annual plan on purpose: most people finish in a few months, and
selling a year to somebody who needs six months is taking money for months they
will not use.

⚠️ Payments are not switched on yet. Nothing is charged today.

## What it will not do

- It will not invent a number. Every figure in a plan traces to one you gave it.
- It will not tell you what you are, what you can handle, or how you feel.
- It will not delay a move to protect your state of mind. Being stuck is usually
  a lack of action, and the plan is action.
- It will not give one instruction where there is a real choice. Where a move
  can be done in materially different ways, it names the ways and the trade-offs
  and leaves the decision with you.
- It is not a coach and does not pretend a person is on the other end.

## How it works

1. Six questions, free, no account — a rough read of where you are
2. The full intake, then your map: three moves, the gates, what was crossed off
   and why, what it took as given
3. Optional: the walkthrough for the move you are standing on
