# Service Call SOP — Bridgewater Mechanical
Version 2.1 · Owner: Marcus Reyes · Reviewed quarterly

## Purpose
The standard commercial service callout, from dispatch to invoice. This is the
job we do roughly fifty times a month and it should not vary by who takes it.

## Before leaving the shop
1. Confirm site access and the on-site contact by phone, not email. A locked
   door is the single most common cause of a wasted callout.
2. Check the equipment history in the job file. If this is a callback on the
   same fault within 90 days, flag it to the lead tech before attending — a
   second visit on the same fault is not billable and needs a different
   approach.
3. Confirm the van has the common parts for that equipment class.

## On site
4. Photograph the nameplate before touching anything. Model and serial save
   an hour of guesswork later and settle most warranty questions.
5. Diagnose fully before starting any repair.
6. **Quote before you fix.** No work proceeds without the customer agreeing to
   the price. If the customer cannot authorise, stop and call the office.
7. Test under load, not just at start-up. A unit that starts is not a unit
   that works.
8. Photograph the completed work.

## After
9. Debrief notes into the job file the same day, while it is fresh.
10. Invoice same day. Every day an invoice sits unissued is a day added to
    collections, and our receivables are already the constraint on the business.

## Escalation
Refrigerant leaks over 10% of charge, any gas work outside ticket scope, and
anything involving a confined space go to the lead tech before proceeding.
See the confined space procedure.
