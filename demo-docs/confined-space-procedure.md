# Confined Space Entry Procedure — Bridgewater Mechanical
Version 3.0 · Owner: Priya Raman · Aligned to WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation Part 9

⚠️ This is our internal procedure. It does not replace the regulation, and
where the two differ the regulation governs. Questions about compliance go to
WorkSafeBC, not to this document.

## Scope
Applies to mechanical rooms below grade, roof plenums with restricted egress,
crawl spaces, and any duct or vessel entry.

## Before entry
1. A written entry permit is required for every entry. No verbal permits.
2. Identify the hazards: atmosphere, engulfment, mechanical, electrical,
   thermal, and configuration.
3. Test the atmosphere in this order — **oxygen, then flammables, then
   toxics.** Record every reading on the permit with the time taken.
4. Acceptable ranges: oxygen 19.5%–23.0%, flammable gas below 10% LEL, carbon
   monoxide below 25 ppm, hydrogen sulphide below 10 ppm.
5. Isolate and lock out all energy sources. Verify at the point of work.
6. Ventilate continuously. Do not rely on a single pre-entry purge.

## During entry
7. An attendant remains outside for the full duration. The attendant does not
   take on other work and does not leave, for any reason.
8. Continuous atmospheric monitoring where conditions can change.
9. Communication check every 5 minutes.
10. Retrieval equipment rigged and attached before entry where vertical.

## Emergency
11. **The attendant does not enter to rescue.** Most confined-space fatalities
    are would-be rescuers. Call emergency services and use retrieval equipment
    from outside.
12. Rescue plan is confirmed before entry, not improvised during one.

## Records
Permits are retained for three years. Atmospheric readings form part of the
permit and are not recorded separately.
