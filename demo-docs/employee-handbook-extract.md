# Employee Handbook — extract
Bridgewater Mechanical · Effective January 2026

## Hours and overtime
Standard week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday. Overtime is paid at 1.5× after 8
hours in a day or 40 in a week, in line with BC Employment Standards.

⚠️ Overtime has been running heavily on the service side for roughly nine
months and is concentrated on two technicians. This is a known issue and is on
the roadmap for review. It is noted here because the handbook should not
describe a policy the business is not actually following.

## On-call rotation
Service techs rotate weekend on-call. On-call carries a flat weekly stipend
plus callout rates for any attendance. The rotation is published a month ahead.

## Tickets and certification
The company pays for gas ticket renewal and refrigerant handling certification,
and for the time taken to sit them. Tickets must be current — a lapsed ticket
means the technician cannot be dispatched on that work, which is a scheduling
problem for everyone.

## Tools
The company provides specialised tools and test equipment. Technicians provide
their own hand tools. Damaged company equipment is replaced without deduction
unless there is clear misuse.

## Vehicles
Company vehicles are for company use. Personal use outside commuting requires
approval. Fuel cards are for company vehicles only.

## Raising a problem
Speak to your lead first if that is comfortable, and to the owner if it is not.
Nobody is disadvantaged for raising something in good faith.
