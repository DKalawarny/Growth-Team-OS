# Estimating & Quoting SOP — Bridgewater Mechanical
Version 1.4 · DRAFT — 65% complete · Owner: Jim Hollis

⚠️ This document is incomplete. Sections 5 and 6 are unwritten. It is the
active bottleneck on the margin problem.

## Why this exists
Margin fell from 11% to 7% while revenue grew 18%. The working theory is that
quotes are being built off material rates that predate the last two supplier
increases, so we are winning more work at prices that no longer carry the cost
of delivering it.

## 1. Scope before price
Never price from a phone description. Site visit or drawings, always.

## 2. Labour
Use planned hours from the job-costing history for the equipment class, not a
gut number. Willow Creek phase one ran 20% over planned hours and nobody knew
until invoicing — that variance should be visible during the job, not after.

## 3. Materials
Pull current rates from the supplier price file. **The rate file must be no
more than 30 days old.** If it is older, refresh before quoting.

## 4. Margin floor
No commercial install quote goes out below 22% gross. Service work floor is
35% gross. If a job cannot clear the floor, it is a decision for the owner and
not for the estimator.
