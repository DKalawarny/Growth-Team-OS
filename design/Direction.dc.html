Design direction
Cool, quiet, alive.
A near-white canvas with one living green. Faith shows in the language and the structure — never in the ornament. Deliberately unlike kinwove: cooler, sharper, more current.

If green isn't it — two alternates
Clear blue · #2E7DE0
Reads more financial and more neutral. Safest in front of a banker, least distinctive.

Cooler and more clinical than the green. Fresh, slightly more tech.
Living green · #14A67B — drawn here
Life and growth without saying either word. Most alive of the three.
