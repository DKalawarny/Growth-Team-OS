# the way out — where the people are

Two routes, and they are not equal. Written down 23 Sep at Daniel's request:
*"i like that last idea note that and how we can market to those people. also
this might be a more searched thing being stuck what could it do."*

---

## 1. The partner route — one relationship beats twelve months of pages

**The pitch, in one line: your clients leave with a plan instead of a pamphlet.**

These are organisations that already have a book of stuck people, are already
paid, and have nothing good to hand them at the end of a conversation. They do
not want a referral fee. They want the person to be better off and off their
desk, and they want to look competent doing it.

Ranked by concentration of the exact person this is built for:

| Partner | Why them | The opening |
|---|---|---|
| **Licensed insolvency trustees** (Canada) | Legally mandated to deliver two counselling sessions. Every single client is stuck, by definition, and the trustee has to fill that hour | "Your two sessions currently end with advice. They could end with a written plan they keep" |
| **Credit counselling agencies** (non-profit) | Same population, mission-aligned, no profit motive to protect. Often government or bank funded | Mission fit — they can say yes without a commercial argument |
| **Unions** — locals with member assistance | Members in trades and shift work; the local is trusted in a way no app is | One email from the local beats a thousand impressions |
| **Employers of shift workers** — trades, trucking, healthcare, warehousing | Financial stress is an attendance and turnover problem they already pay for. EAP budgets exist and are underspent | "Your night shift is the hardest to retain. This is what your people are actually trying to solve" |
| **Churches** | Many already run financial ministries. Daniel has standing here | ⚠️ Keep it a separate conversation from kinwove — see the note at the bottom |
| **Licensed insolvency / divorce / estate lawyers** | See people at the exact life transition this product is about | They need something to give, not another referral out |
| **Realtors** | A house sale IS move one for a whole class of user | The net-sheet conversation is already theirs |

### How the money works there — NOT $29 a head

⚠️ Consumer pricing does not survive contact with this route. A trustee is not
going to ask a client in insolvency for $29 a month, and should not.

Shape it as **the organisation pays, the person does not**: a flat monthly per
office or per counsellor, or a block of plans bought up front. Their alternative
is a photocopied budget worksheet, so the bar is low and the value obvious.
⭐ It also fixes the hardest problem in the consumer model — **a person whose
problem is money paying monthly for help with money.** Here somebody else pays.

### What to have ready before the first conversation

1. **One real plan, anonymised.** Not a demo — an actual map with real numbers.
   Nothing else explains this product as fast.
2. **The terms.** A trustee or a union cannot hand members a tool whose terms
   belong to a different company. This is the hard blocker.
3. **A name and a domain.** "It's at eliv8os.com/wayout" ends the meeting.
4. **An answer to "what happens to their data."** These bodies are regulated and
   it is the second question every time. ⭐ The answer is already good —
   migration 046 scopes every row to the PERSON, never a company, deliberately
   so an employer can never see an employee's exit plan. Say that out loud.

## 2. The search route — reconsidered, upward

🔴 **I FIRST ESTIMATED THIS OFF KINWOVE'S NUMBERS AND THAT WAS THE WRONG
BENCHMARK.** Kinwove has produced four stranger signups in its life from
AI/SEO — but it is a Bible study app competing with YouVersion's 600 million
installs. "Stuck" is not that category.

**The head terms are enormous and unwinnable.** "How to get out of debt" and
"I hate my job" are among the most commercially contested phrases on the
internet — NerdWallet, Ramsey, Reddit, banks, and debt consolidators paying $50
a click. Do not go there.

⭐⭐ **THE OPPORTUNITY IS THE SHAPE OF THE QUESTION, NOT THE VOLUME OF THE
KEYWORD.** What this product answers is not a keyword at all:

> *"I'm 58, my mother left me a house, I work nights and I want to stop."*

No page ranks for that because it is not a search — it is a situation. But it is
exactly what people now type into an assistant, and Daniel's own data across two
products says the same thing: **the only channel that has ever delivered a
stranger is an AI assistant.** An assistant recommending a tool is a completely
different game from ranking first, and long, specific, situational questions are
the ones assistants answer and search engines never served.

### So the answer pages should be SITUATIONS, not topics

  ❌ "How to get out of debt"          (unwinnable, and low intent anyway)
  ✅ "What to do with an inherited house when you still have credit card debt"
  ✅ "Can I stop working nights if I sell the house"
  ✅ "I'm 55 with no pension and one asset"
  ✅ "Should I pay off the truck or invest the money"

Each one is a plan this product already knows how to write. ⚠️ And each page has
to END somewhere — the mistake to avoid is an answer page so complete that
nobody needs the map.

### What that changes

At 5,000 visitors a month by month twelve — plausible with 50 situation pages in
a category this size, not plausible for kinwove in its category — the funnel runs
roughly: **15% start the intake · 40% finish it · 5–8% subscribe** ≈ 18 new
subscribers a month, ~110 paying at once, ~$3,200 a month exiting year one.

⚠️ **The 40% is the softest number and the biggest lever in the whole business.**
The intake is **37 questions and says "about 15 minutes"**. Every one is a place
to quit. Halving that filter moves the year-one result more than any referral
scheme, any price change, and most of the pages.

## ⚠️ Do not let these two blur

**Churches are a kinwove relationship and this is a different product.** Walking
into a church with two products confuses both and spends trust that belongs to
neither. If the way out goes to churches it goes on its own name, with its own
terms, as its own thing.
